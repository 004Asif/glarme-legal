1. Who we are

The App is published by ADot.Interactive and developed by 004asif. You can reach us at bravo.charlie1492@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Guest mode (no account)

Glarme works fully without an account. In guest mode your alarms, settings and statistics (streaks, challenges solved, wake-up times) are stored only on your device. We do not upload guest data. If you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, guest data is lost.

Optional account

If you create an account, sign-in is handled by Supabase authentication using your email address and a password. Account emails may include verification and password-reset messages. With an account we store:

  • Email address and display name;
  • Profile stats: wake-up streak, daily app-usage streak, total challenges solved, level, title, average wake-up time, and pro status.

This data is stored in a hosted cloud database provided by Supabase (see section 7).

Public leaderboard

The leaderboard is visible to all App users, including guests. It shows your display name, title, level, wake-up streak, app streak and challenges solved. You choose your display name. We recommend you do not use your real name if you prefer not to be identifiable.

Data kept only on your device

Alarms, alarm settings, sound choices and app settings are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Advertising data

The free version of the App is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may process an advertising identifier, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions. See section 5.

Purchase information

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing and managed through RevenueCat. We receive purchase status and an anonymous app user identifier. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

What we do not collect

We do not add analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own. The advertising SDK described in section 5 may collect diagnostic data (such as crash and performance logs) on its own. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone data. We do not sell personal data.

3. How we use information

  • To run the App, including sign-in, account features, your profile and the leaderboard;
  • To keep streaks and statistics consistent across your devices when you have an account;
  • To show and measure ads in the free version, and to personalise them only where permitted;
  • To manage subscriptions and unlock purchased features;
  • To protect the App, prevent abuse, and keep the leaderboard fair;
  • To respond to support requests and to meet legal obligations.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract - providing account features, profile and leaderboard, and purchases;
  • Consent - personalised advertising, which is only enabled where you agree (including through the consent form described below);
  • Legitimate interests - security, preventing abuse, and keeping the leaderboard fair.

5. Advertising

Ads in the free version are provided by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, app interactions (including ad interactions), and diagnostics such as crash and performance logs, to serve, measure, secure and, where allowed, personalise ads. Glarme ads are rated at most PG and are not directed at children (see section 11).

  • Consent form. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form (provided through the User Messaging Platform) is shown so you can choose how ads are personalised. You can change this later in the App under Settings > "Ad privacy options", where that option is available.
  • "Personalized ads" switch. In Settings > Privacy you can turn off "Personalized ads". When it is off, ad requests are sent as non-personalised.
  • Google's privacy information for ads is available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements. RevenueCat's privacy information is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Refunds and cancellations are governed by Google Play's policies (see the Terms of Use).

7. Service providers and international transfers

  • Supabase - authentication and hosted database for accounts and profile stats. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob - advertising. See section 5.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat - purchases. See section 6.

These providers may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA, UK or Switzerland. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards these providers make available for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Device permissions

  • Exact alarms - to ring at the time you set;
  • Notifications - to show alarm and account-related messages;
  • Full-screen intent - to show the alarm over the lock screen;
  • Vibration - for alarm vibration;
  • Boot completed - to reschedule your alarms after the device restarts;
  • Internet - for accounts, leaderboard, purchases and ads.

The App does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

9. Data retention

  • Account data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account in the App removes it immediately; email deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Guest data stays on your device only, and is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Device-only alarm and settings data stays on your device until you change or delete it.
  • Ad and purchase records are kept by Google and RevenueCat under their own policies.

10. Security

Data sent between the App and our providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Session tokens are stored encrypted on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

Glarme is not directed at children under 13, and our ads are not child-directed (ad requests are tagged as not child-directed and not subject to the age-of-consent tag). If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact bravo.charlie1492@gmail.com.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • export your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including personalised advertising.

EEA and UK (GDPR)

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents may request to know what personal information we collect, to correct it, and to delete it. We do not sell personal information for money. When personalized ads are on, Google AdMob may use your advertising ID and ad interactions for cross-context behavioural advertising, which California law may treat as "sharing". You can opt out at any time by turning off Settings > Privacy > Personalized ads in the app (ads then become non-personalized), or by resetting or deleting your device advertising ID in Android settings. You may use the contact details below to make a request; we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, email bravo.charlie1492@gmail.com from the email address on your account where possible.

13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data in the App: go to Settings > Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is immediate and removes your profile, stats, leaderboard entry and sign-in. If you no longer have the App, email bravo.charlie1492@gmail.com from your account email with the subject "Delete my account"; we delete account data within 30 days. Full steps, including what is not deleted, are on the Data Collection & Deletion page. Guest data on your device can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.
