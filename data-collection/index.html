Guest mode and device-only data

Without an account, Glarme keeps everything on your device: alarms, alarm settings, sound choices, app settings, and guest statistics. This data is not uploaded. It is lost if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Accounts and the leaderboard

Creating an account is optional. Accounts use email and password sign-in through Supabase. Your profile stats are stored with Supabase and your display name and stats appear on the public leaderboard, which guests can also see.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may also collect app-interaction data and diagnostics (such as crash and performance logs) to run and secure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you may see Google's consent form. You can change ad choices later in the App under "Ad privacy options" (where available), and you can turn off "Personalized ads" in Settings > Privacy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat manages entitlements and receives purchase status with an anonymous app user identifier.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and the data we hold for it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1: delete in the App (immediate)

  1. Open Glarme and go to Settings > Delete account.
  2. Type DELETE to confirm.
  3. Your account is deleted immediately. This removes your profile, your stats, your leaderboard entry, and your sign-in.

Option 2: request deletion by email (if you no longer have the App)

  1. Open an email app and start a new message to support@glarme.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. Send the email from the same address you used to create your Glarme account, so we can verify the request.
  4. We will confirm receipt and delete your account data within 30 days. Account data includes your email address, display name, and profile stats. It is then removed from the leaderboard.

What deletion does not remove

  • Alarms and app settings stay on your device. Account deletion does not touch them. To remove them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Guest data is stored only on your device, so we cannot delete it for you. Clear the App's data or uninstall the App to remove it.
  • Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play are governed by Google's policies.
